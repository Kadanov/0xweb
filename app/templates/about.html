About 0xweb

0xweb is a deliberately vulnerable web-application penetration-testing lab built for methodical, hands-on security training.

  • 13 vulnerability categories, each with 3 difficulty levels (?level=1|2|3).
  • 39 signed, per-deployment flags — a scoreboard that proves you actually exploited each level.
  • Realistic bypasses: filter evasion, out-of-band, cookie theft, Host-header routing, and more.
